# Making a sample calculation of Kalketa

A brief for whoever turns a prepared calculation into a sample shipped with the program. Read `reference/README.md` first, then look at two finished samples as models: `reference/13_Geodezie_a_geoinformatika/R31-rajon` and `reference/08_Materialove_inzenyrstvi/R27-zkouska-tahem`.

Other people work in the same tree at the same time. Create only files inside your new case folder. Never edit an existing file, never commit, never run `git add`.

## What a finished case holds

In `reference/<field>/[<subfield>/]R<n>-<short-name>/`:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `<name>.cc`, `<name>.en.cc` | the sheet, Czech and English; everything but the words is identical |
| `<name>.expected.json` | the results both sheets must reproduce: `sheet` (the Czech file), `verifiedBy` (how they were verified, at least a sentence), `relativeTolerance`, `values`, `checks` with Czech labels |
| `podklady.md`, `podklady.en.md` | the theory in your own words, with at least one ` ```kalk ` block that Kalketa checks |
| `kontrola.md`, `kontrola.en.md` | the calculation again by another way in Python, ending in `result("name", value, "unit")` lines |
| `images/kresli.py`, `images/<x>.svg`, `images/<x>.en.svg` | our own drawing, generated by the script; `.en.svg` only when the picture holds words |
| `layout.json` | `{"headings": "numbered"}` |

## The sheet

- The first heading says **what is calculated**, not who or what the story is about.
- Then a paragraph: "Podle … (source). <the task in our own words>", and the line `"Zdroj: <url>"` (English: `"Source: <authors, title, url>"`). Say what is assumed where the source says nothing.
- **SI units and SI prefixes** only. Old units may stay in a comment. Names of quantities as the source has them.
- Sections `## Zadání`, then calculation in steps as on paper, then checks. A chart where it shows something a number does not. A short sentence on what the results mean at the end.
- Compare with the source's result in words where they differ, and say why. If the source has an error, say so plainly.

## English

Translate only words: title, headings, quoted paragraphs, comments after an apostrophe, labels of `check "…"`, `title:`, `xlabel:`, `ylabel:` strings, image alt texts, Markdown prose, comments and printed strings in Python, words in SVG. Never change names of variables and functions, formulas, numbers in calculation lines, units, line order, `{…}` attributes, ` ```kalk ` blocks, table names and keys, `result("…")` names. Decimal point in English prose. Plain natural technical English, the terms an English textbook uses. Program words: check (posudek), Sources layer, "Check in Python" layer, Database layer, File → Properties, Sample calculations.

Small Czech letters are allowed in an English file only inside `source "…"`, on a line with `Source:`, in the unit Kč and in names of the calculation. Titles and authors of Czech sources go on the `Source:` line, as written.

## Copyright

Use the numbers and the task of a source, not its text or its figures. Write everything in your own words and draw your own pictures. If a source's licence or note forbids this kind of use, stop and report it instead of making the sample.

## Verify

From the repository root:

```bash
node scripts/sample-check.mjs R<n>
```

Then look at your pictures, as tests see numbers only: `node scripts/sample-pictures.mjs R<n>` writes a PNG and says where; read it and fix any label that is cut off at the edge or runs into a line.

Fix your files until it says OK. Never change a test, an expected value to fit a wrong result, or anything outside your folder. If Kalketa itself cannot do something or computes something wrong, do not work around it silently: report it.

## Report

Briefly: files made, how the results compare with the source, assumptions, source errors found, anything Kalketa could not do, and anything you are unsure of.
